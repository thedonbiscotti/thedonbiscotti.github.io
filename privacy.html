Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

This policy covers the Don Biscotti uploader (the "tool"), a private tool the creator of the Don Biscotti series uses to publish the series' videos to its own accounts.

What the tool accesses

The tool signs in only to the series' own accounts. With that permission it uploads videos and captions, and reads basic details such as the channel or account name, and the status and statistics of the series' own videos.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens and upload records are stored only on the creator's own computer. They are not stored on this website, and this website uses no cookies or analytics.

What it shares

Videos and captions are sent to the platform they are being published on. Nothing is sold, and no data is shared with anyone else or used for advertising. The tool does not collect personal information about viewers or other users.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at Google's security settings.

Deleting data

To have data removed or a connection revoked, write to don.biscotti7@gmail.com. Stored tokens and records are deleted within 30 days. Connections can also be removed in the security or app settings of each platform.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: don.biscotti7@gmail.com
