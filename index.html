Don Biscotti

Don Biscotti is a fictional comedy series of short AI-generated videos about a tiny French bulldog puppy who runs a mafia family from under the kitchen table.

The Don Biscotti uploader

The Don Biscotti uploader is a private tool used only by the series' creator. It publishes the series' own finished videos, with their captions, to the series' own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. It does not offer accounts to anyone else, and it does not read or collect information about viewers.
